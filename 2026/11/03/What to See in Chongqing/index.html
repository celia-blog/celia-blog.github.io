What to See in Chongqing: Top Attractions for First‑Time Visitors

Chongqing is a famous mountain city in southwest China. Built on hills, many of its streets, buildings and walkways sit on different vertical levels. If you are planning a short trip to Chongqing and wondering what to see in Chongqing, you will find plenty of accessible attractions to explore, with wonderful views to enjoy both day and night.

Hongyadong

Hongyadong is one of the most famous Chongqing attractions. It is well‑known for traditional stilt‑style architecture, layered buildings and dazzling night‑time lighting. Countless visitors come here to take photos and experience the distinctive charm of this mountain city.

Yangtze River

The Yangtze River runs through Chongqing. A walk along the riverside or a river cruise lets you soak in relaxing river views of the city. After dark, riverside lights reflect beautifully across the water, making it a perfect spot for slow sightseeing.

Yangtze River Cable Car

The Yangtze River Cable Car offers a special way to cross the river. It has been in operation for decades, and riders can enjoy sweeping views of the river and cityscape below. It is a popular experience for travellers exploring Chongqing.

Final Thoughts

When you plan what to see in Chongqing, these landmark spots are great starting points for your itinerary. Whether you prefer slow, casual walks or lively scenic spots, Chongqing delivers unique views you cannot find in other Chinese cities.
