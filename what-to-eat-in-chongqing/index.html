What to Eat in Chongqing: Local Food Guide for Travellers

Created2026-11-03|Updated2026-11-08|Chongqing Travel
|Post Views:

Chongqing cuisine is famous for its bold, spicy flavours. If you want to know what to eat in Chongqing, Chongqing hot pot is the most iconic local dish, loved by locals and travellers alike.

Hot Pot

Chongqing hot pot uses rich, spicy broth filled with chilli oil and Sichuan peppercorns. You can cook meat, vegetables, tofu and noodles inside the boiling pot. The flavour is numbing and spicy, a signature taste of the city.

Spicy Noodles

Spicy noodles are a cheap and tasty daily food for many locals. A simple bowl of noodles tossed in chilli sauce, garlic and spices can bring a wonderful burst of flavour. It is a must‑try when exploring local food in Chongqing.

Local Snacks

There are many kinds of street snacks across the city. Fried stinky tofu, spicy tofu pudding and crispy meat are popular picks for visitors looking to try small bites.

Food Tips for Visitors

Many local restaurants stay open late, especially near popular shopping and entertainment areas. Prices can vary depending on the ingredients chosen. Hot pot usually costs more than noodles or street snacks.
If you are travelling alone, noodles and snacks may be easier to order than hot pot. If you are with friends, hot pot offers a fuller Chongqing food experience. Do not rush through the meal, because spicy food can take time to digest.

After sightseeing at famous landmarks, you can combine delicious local food with the top attractions in Chongqing for a wonderful itinerary.

Final Thoughts

Trying local dishes is one of the best ways to experience Chongqing culture. When deciding what to eat in Chongqing, hot pot, spicy noodles and street snacks are classic picks for every traveller.
