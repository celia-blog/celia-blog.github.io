What to Eat in Chongqing: Local Food Guide for Travellers

Created2026-11-03|Updated2026-11-10|Chongqing Travel
|Post Views:

Chongqing cuisine is famous for its bold, spicy flavours. If you want to know what to eat in Chongqing, Chongqing hot pot is the most iconic local dish, loved by locals and travellers alike.

Hot Pot

Chongqing hot pot uses rich, spicy broth filled with chilli oil and Sichuan peppercorns. You can cook meat, vegetables, tofu and noodles inside the boiling pot. The flavour is numbing and spicy, a signature taste of the city.

Spicy Noodles

Spicy noodles are a cheap and tasty daily food for many locals. Fresh noodles tossed in chilli oil, garlic and spices deliver an explosion of flavour. This is an absolute must-try.

Local Snacks

There are many kinds of street snacks across the city. Spicy tofu pudding and crispy fried meat are crowd favourites among visitors wanting to sample small local bites.

Food Tips for Visitors

Many local restaurants stay open late, especially around popular shopping and entertainment districts. Prices vary based on the ingredients you order. Hot pot is generally more expensive than noodles or street snacks.
If you travel solo, noodles and street snacks are much easier to order than hot pot. When travelling with friends, hot pot delivers a more immersive taste of Chongqing cuisine. Take your time while eating; spicy dishes require more time for digestion.
After exploring iconic landmarks, pair tasty local food with the top attractions in Chongqing to build a fantastic travel itinerary.

Final Thoughts

Sampling local dishes is one of the best ways to dive into Chongqing’s culture. When planning what to eat in Chongqing, hot pot, spicy noodles and street food are classic must-tries for all travellers.
